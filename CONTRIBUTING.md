# Contributing to Oak National Academy projects

Thank you for taking an interest in Oak National Academy's open-source work.
This is the organisation-wide default contributing guide. If the repository
you are reading has its own `CONTRIBUTING.md`, that one applies instead —
some of our projects have their own routes for feedback and their own
conventions.

## Are pull requests accepted?

**Not from outside Oak National Academy at the moment.** Our repositories back
production services used by teachers and pupils, so changes go through Oak's
internal review and release process. We are not able to review or merge
external pull requests, and we would rather say so plainly than leave one
sitting unanswered.

This position is under review — we hope to find an approach that works for us
and the community, and a repository's own `CONTRIBUTING.md` may say otherwise.

## What feedback helps most

We very much want to hear from people using our code, our APIs and our
curriculum data. The most useful things you can tell us are:

- **Bugs** — something behaving differently from what the documentation says.
  Include what you did, what you expected, and what actually happened.
- **Data problems** — curriculum content that looks wrong, incomplete or out
  of date.
- **Documentation that is wrong or unclear** — in any of our repositories.
- **Accessibility barriers** — anything in our documentation, sites or tools
  that is not accessible to you. Include any assistive technology you were
  using.
- **Questions and ideas** — what you are trying to build, and what is missing.

## How to send feedback

1. **Open an issue on the repository** you are reading, if issues are enabled
   there.
2. If issues are disabled, the repository's own `README.md` or `SUPPORT.md`
   says where feedback for that project goes.
3. For anything else, or if you are not sure where to start, contact
   [Oak support](https://support.thenational.academy/).

## Security

Please do **not** report security vulnerabilities through public issues, pull
requests or discussions. Follow our
[security policy](https://github.com/oaknational/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md)
instead, which points to Oak's
[security.txt](https://www.thenational.academy/.well-known/security.txt).

## For Oak engineers

If you work at Oak: branch from `main`, follow
[Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/), and open a pull
request — every change needs a passing build and an approving review before it
merges. Each repository's own documentation covers its specific setup, test
and release process.

## Code of conduct

Everyone interacting with Oak's projects — through issues, feedback or code —
is expected to follow our
[Code of Conduct](https://github.com/oaknational/.github/blob/main/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
